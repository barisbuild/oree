A few things
to know.

Does it replace Finder?

No. Oree stands in for Finder's desktop, the layer behind your windows. Finder itself keeps running, your files stay where they are, and "Show in Finder" still opens Finder.

What does the assistant need?

Claude Code installed and signed in on your Mac. Oree drives it and holds no keys of its own. Without it, the widgets and files still work.

Can the assistant change my files?

Yes, and it asks first. Oree shows you each change with Allow and Don't allow before it is made. If you would rather it did not ask, Bypass permissions lets it act on its own, once you have accepted the risk.

Are my conversations kept?

Yes, on your Mac, in Oree's own folder. They come back when Oree starts again, and the agent picks each one up where it left off.

Can the picture fill the screen?

Yes. In Settings, choose Full screen and the picture runs to every edge, with the widgets as glass over it. At the top, it fades into the desktop instead.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Oree is open source under the MIT licence. The assistant uses your own Claude plan.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 or later. The glass surfaces need macOS 26; earlier versions get a frosted material instead.

How do I install it?

For now you build it from source with one command, ./run.sh. Xcode's command line tools are enough.
